Your Friday check in

How to measure.

Weight and measurements happen once a week, every Friday morning. I don’t want you weighing every day, because it fluctuates like crazy. Once a week, and we look at the trend over weeks and months.

MeasurementsProgress photos

Before you start

  1. First thing Friday morning
  2. After you’ve been to the toilet
  3. Before you’ve eaten or drunk anything
  4. Weigh yourself first, then measure
Where to measure
  1. 1
    WaistStraight across your belly button.
  2. 2
    HipsThe widest point around your bum. Feet together.
  3. 3
    Upper thighHalfway between the crease at the top of your leg and the top of your kneecap. Always the same leg.
  4. 4
    Upper armHalfway between your shoulder and your elbow, arm relaxed by your side. Always the same arm.

You don’t need to do your chest. If your scales give you a body fat reading, pop that in too. No worries if they don’t.

Get it right every time
  • Tape flat against your skin, snug but not digging in.
  • Keep the tape level all the way round.
  • Stand relaxed and breathe normally. Don’t suck in.
  • Same spot, same time, same conditions, every Friday.
  • Pop the numbers straight into your check in form.

Progress photos (optional)

You don’t have to do these. I never force them on anyone, because I know it can be quite a daunting thing to do. But they’re really useful when the scale and the measurements aren’t shifting like we’d want them to, because the photos often show changes the numbers don’t.

If you want to do them, it’s every 2 to 4 weeks. Not every week.

The 3 photos
How to take them
  1. Friday morning, same conditions as your weigh in.
  2. Stand against a plain wall, the same one every time.
  3. Wear the same fitted clothing every time.
  4. Same lighting, same spot, same distance.
  5. Prop your phone up at waist height, 2 to 3 big steps away, and use the timer. Or get someone to take them for you.
  6. Front, side, then back. Arms relaxed by your sides, feet hip width apart, standing tall. Head to toe in the frame.
  7. Pop them in the progress photos section of the app. Only I see them.
Not sure about something? Message me before Friday and we’ll sort it. Getting it right once means every number after it actually means something.
